Popover popover=hint Behavior Tests

Tests for whatwg/html #12345 — model implementation: Jake Archibald demo (source), WPT PR #59237.

Key hierarchy rule: For a hint to coexist with an open auto, the hint must be triggered from a button that lives inside the open auto (or hint) popover, with showPopover({source: thisButton}). The browser traces the source element's popover ancestry to establish the parent–child relationship. Two floating, independent popovers with unrelated trigger buttons have no relationship and the stack dismisses one when the other opens.

GREEN = auto popover / button YELLOW = hint popover / button RED = manual popover / button GREY = hide/reset

Browser compatibility — whatwg/html #12345 (API-level probe)

API PASS/FAIL = imperative JS test. Browsers may pass API tests while interactive behaviour (hover, click, light-dismiss) still uses old rules. Always confirm with the manual scenarios below.

Scenario 1 (Rule 1): Showing a hint from inside an auto does not close the auto

The hint trigger button lives inside the auto popover. showPopover({source: thisButton}) tells the browser the button's open ancestor (the auto) is the hint's parent. Both stay visible simultaneously.

Scenario 2 (Rule 2): Opening a hint closes non-ancestor hints but not ancestor hints

Step 1: open the ancestor hint. Step 2: open Hint A from a button inside the ancestor — making A a child of ancestor. Step 3: open Hint B from another button inside the ancestor — Hint A closes (sibling = non-ancestor of B), but the ancestor hint stays (it IS an ancestor of B).

Then use the buttons inside the ancestor hint.

I am the ancestor. I should stay open throughout.
S2-Hint A
I close when Hint B opens — we are siblings, so I am a non-ancestor of B.
S2-Hint B
Hint A closed. The ancestor hint is still open.

Scenario 3 (Rule 3): Clicking outside closes auto and hint consistently

Open the auto, click the button inside to show the hint (it stays open — no hover dismiss). Both are now visible. Test what clicking in different places does.

After showing both: click outside all popovers → auto and hint close, manual stays.
Click on the hint → hint is a child of auto, so clicking inside hint is NOT outside the auto — auto should stay. Clicking on the auto → that IS outside the hint — hint closes.

S3-Auto
Clicking outside closes me. Clicking on my child hint does NOT close me.
S3-Hint (child of auto)
Clicking on me → auto stays (I'm its child).
Clicking on the auto → I close.
S3-Manual
Click outside me — I should stay open.

Scenario 3b (Rule 3 nested): Hint light-dismisses on click-outside; ancestor auto stays

Auto1 is the root. Auto2 and Hint are both children of Auto1 (triggered from inside it). Clicking outside all popovers → all three close (both auto and hint light-dismiss).
Clicking on Auto1's body (not on Auto2 or Hint) → Auto2 and Hint close, Auto1 stays.

Then use the buttons inside Auto1 to open Auto2 and the hint. Once all three are open, click in different areas to see what closes.

S3b-Auto1 (root ancestor)
Click anywhere on me (not on Auto2 or Hint) → my children close, I stay.
S3b-Auto2 (child of Auto1)
S3b-Hint (child of Auto1)
I light-dismiss on click-outside, just like an auto.

Scenario 4 (Rule 4): Hiding an auto does not close an unrelated hint

Opens both the auto and the unrelated hint together, then hides the auto after 4 seconds via a timer — no clicks needed, so light-dismiss cannot interfere. The hint should still be visible after the auto disappears.
Why a timer? Clicking "hide auto" is itself a click outside both open popovers, which light-dismisses them both before the button's handler runs. A timer hides the auto programmatically with no user click involved.

S4-Auto (unrelated to hint)

Scenario 5 (Rule 5): Auto shown as child of hint is downgraded to hint

When an auto is shown with a source element that is inside an open hint, its effective type is downgraded to hint. Two observable effects: (A) it does not close an already-open unrelated auto (a real auto would); and (B) an unrelated hint does close it (because it is now in the hint stack).

Then use the buttons inside each popover in order.

S5-Hint (child of Auto1)
S5-Auto2 (should be downgraded to hint)
If Auto1 is still open → downgrade worked (I didn't close it).
S5-Unrelated Hint
Auto2 should have closed when I appeared (it's in the hint stack).
